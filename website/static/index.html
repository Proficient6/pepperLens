CONVOLUTION IN ACTION

The Gaussian is our low-pass filter.

A normalized 3 × 3 Gaussian kernel, with σ = 0.7 pixels, forms a weighted neighbourhood sum. It attenuates high spatial frequencies before HSV thresholding and texture extraction. The filter-response plot connects the spatial operation to its frequency-domain behaviour.

y[m,n] = Σᵢ Σⱼ h[i,j] · x[m−i,n−j]

Image frequency is measured in cycles per pixel, with Nyquist at 0.5. Sobel edges and Fourier spectra are visual diagnostics; they do not add classifier features. BGR↔RGB reorders channels, while RGB→HSV transforms the colour coordinates.
